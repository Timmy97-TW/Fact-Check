## Alarming things noticed in the raw files

1. **Set 5 NaCl masses are internally ~25% high** (PA-014 above). This is the single most
   consequential arithmetic problem in the slice: it affects every set 5 trial 1 and trial 2 value
   the wiki quotes as 150 and 250 mM.
2. **The set 7 "trial 4" chlorophyll sheet (95% acetone, coefficients 6.63 / 18.08, 665 / 649 nm)
   has no basis in any notebook or protocol.** Every logged extraction, all year, is 80% acetone
   read at 645 and 663 nm. Someone switched to a different published equation without recording it.
3. **The 9 Aug set 7 fresh weight table appears twice in the August ELN with a different control:**
   0.1537 g in one copy (also the plant notebook) and 0.1587 g in the other. Same 23 plants.
4. **The 14 May remake table for set 1 is garbled**: a column headed "500mM NaCl" that carries the
   50 mM mass (0.1461 g), a "control" column that also carries 0.1461 g of NaCl, and two trehalose
   volumes (25 and 250 µL) for five columns. If set 1's pre-test table is being read off these
   plates, the group labels themselves are unreliable — relevant background for PA-036.
5. **The protectant protocol's own plate list does not match its procedure**: §B lists 13 plate
   rows and then says "24 agar plates total", and autoclaves at 121 °C while every other agar
   recipe in the lab uses 127 °C.
6. **The 20 July set 5 trial 2 transfer says "using seeds from 0719"** — but the 19 July sowing
   entry is set 6/7 vernalization, and seeds sown on 19 July could not be germinated seedlings on
   20 July. One of those two dates is wrong.
